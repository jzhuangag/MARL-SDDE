## Later async research is not new positive learning evidence

The amortized complete learning study remains NO_GO against its strongest
registered fresh/online controls. The newer sealed-bridge complete native
integration passed6+6 operational cells in56.875 CPU seconds, but collected
no evaluation episodes and supplies no admissible return superiority.
The new prefix-transport/backend implementation is mathematical/software
progress, not scientific performance. None may be added to V3 as confirmed
multi-environment asynchronous actor improvement.

The original V3's proved primary object is a common affine value parameter
updated from multiple Markov actors with correlation and delay. Its controller
jointly selects participation, physical spacing and step size for finite-budget
value-estimation risk. The SDDE motivates drift geometry; the exact discrete
Lyapunov recursion supplies the finite-time guarantee. A moving-policy critic
tracking interface and nonlinear policy-learning experiments do not turn that
guarantee into asynchronous distinct-policy actor return dominance.

## Recommendation / uncertainty

TSP-first is the better fit to the existing coherent contribution and confirmed
evidence. Frame it as finite-budget resource control for delayed correlated
multi-agent Markov value learning, not a generic asynchronous MARL actor solver.
The real enhancements are the finite-horizon objective, the information-versus-
learning-value distinction, and conservative safety separated from performance
ranking. Do not pad V3 with unconfirmed ICML controller prototypes.

Before submission, audit the main discrete theorem/assumptions and the tightest
related-work comparison; align every empirical method with its actual proved
model/interface. A focused delay-by-policy-motion critic-tracking study could
be a useful future addition IF independently registered and positive, but no
such result is asserted to exist now. A full new actor-correction claim still
requires complete strong-baseline learning development and confirmation even
if the intended venue is TSP. ICML readiness is not established.

This is an AI-assisted research assessment, not a prediction of acceptance.
