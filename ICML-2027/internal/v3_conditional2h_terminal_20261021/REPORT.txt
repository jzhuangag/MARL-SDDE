ONE FULL-MECHANISM DIAGNOSIS AND BOUNDED DESIGN JUDGMENT
The robust conditional rule optimizes the continuing program after a feasible
paid pilot. It does not test whether buying that pilot beats the best
full-budget fixed program. Lost training horizon dominates at small budgets.
Within that already-paid pilot class, current-state correction changes almost
none of the aggregate risks; it has no confirmed incremental benefit over the
posterior-marginal rule. This is consistent with, but does not empirically prove,
the pre-outcome second-order state-value ceiling. The known-error oracle adds
no confirmed deployable advantage and is not used as positive evidence.
Stop this state-selection validation avenue on this grid: no tuning, extra
seeds, changed thresholds, retry or selective high-budget primary claim.
