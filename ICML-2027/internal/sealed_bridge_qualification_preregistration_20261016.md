## Decision boundary

Even a complete pass establishes only native integration, not useful precision,
positive return, strong-baseline superiority, cross-family performance or
ICML-readiness. Complete learning development requires a separate fresh-seed
registration, exact native-budget comparison, strong fresh/online/nearest-method
controls, no-transport/fixed-allocation/no-price ablations and sufficiently long
task-specific horizons. This workflow does not automatically launch performance
data. It closes a genuine complete new update mechanism, not another estimator-only
test series. Prior scientific gates are not changed.
