\section{A Finite-Budget Separation with Covariance Uncertainty}
\label{sec:finite-robust}
This section establishes the probe certificate and the short-cycle refinement used alongside main Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-robust-separation}.
The detector and commitments remain fixed throughout the parameter box.
The comparison permits every affordable prefix of every resource cycle of period at most three, stop, and observation-independent random mixtures.
There are $16+16^2+16^3=4368$ ordered cycle words over the sixteen participation--spacing pairs.

\subsection{A target-invariant probe with uniform error control}
For the reference covariance $\Sigma_j=\theta_jR_{1/5}+I/8$, the stationary correlation matrix has eigenvalues in $[2/3,3/2]$.
The covariance ratio in every direction is consequently at least
\[
\frac{2(2/3)+1/8}{(1/20)(2/3)+1/8}=\frac{175}{19}.
\]
Congruence by the full-row-rank first-difference matrix preserves this ratio bound for the seventeen-dimensional contrast covariances.
If $\ell\ge175/19$ is a generalized covariance eigenvalue, its Gaussian affinity factor is $[2\sqrt\ell/(1+\ell)]^{1/2}\le4/5$.
Indeed the factor decreases for $\ell>1$, and its squared comparison reduces to the rational inequality $2500\ell\le256(1+\ell)^2$ at $\ell=175/19$.
The reference affinity is therefore at most $(4/5)^{17}<1/40$.
The square-root likelihood-ratio bound gives each reference directional error at most $1/40$.

For a stationary AR(1) correlation matrix the spectral density is proportional to $(1-\lambda^2)/(1-2\lambda\cos\omega+\lambda^2)$.
Its ratio to the reference density has extrema at $\omega=0,\pi$.
Writing $s(\lambda)=(1+\lambda)/(1-\lambda)$, every allowed true common-state covariance relative to the reference is bounded below by
\[
\frac{99}{100}\min\left\{\frac{s(39/200)}{s(1/5)},\frac{s(1/5)}{s(41/200)}\right\}
=\frac{47223}{48200}>1-\frac1{20}
\]
and above by $(101/100)s(41/200)/s(1/5)=24341/23850<1+1/20$.
The Toeplitz quadratic-form representation transfers these spectral bounds to every finite covariance matrix.
Adding the unchanged private noise and projecting by the contrast matrix preserve both Loewner bounds.
Thus each true contrast covariance satisfies $(1-\varepsilon)K_j\preceq\widetilde K_j\preceq(1+\varepsilon)K_j$, with $\varepsilon=1/20$.
The two true persistence parameters need not coincide.

For centered Gaussian laws of dimension $d$, these covariance bounds imply
\[
D_{\rm KL}(\widetilde P_j\|P_j)
\le\frac{d\varepsilon^2}{4(1-\varepsilon)}.
\]
To see this, each whitened eigenvalue $t\in[1-\varepsilon,1+\varepsilon]$ contributes $(t-1-\log t)/2$.
The largest contribution is at $1-\varepsilon$, and $-\varepsilon-\log(1-\varepsilon)\le\varepsilon^2/[2(1-\varepsilon)]$ by its power series.
The classical total-variation inequality $\|P-Q\|_{\rm TV}^2\le D_{\rm KL}(P\|Q)/2$ follows by applying the log-sum inequality to an event and its complement, then using the binary divergence's second derivative $1/[t(1-t)]\ge4$.
For $d=17$, the total variation is at most $\varepsilon\sqrt{17/[8(1-\varepsilon)]}\le3/40$.
Each true directional error of the unchanged reference detector is therefore at most $1/40+3/40=1/10$.
This is a uniform robustness certificate for specified covariance bands, not an estimator of those bands from the eighteen observations.

\subsection{A sharper lower bound over every fixed prefix}
For $\rho=\lambda^b\ge0$ and $N\ge1$, put $v=q^{-1}+\theta(1+\rho)/(1-\rho)$ and $t=2\theta\rho/(1-\rho)^2$.
The exact stationary running-mean risk at $|e|=r$ obeys
\begin{align*}
R(N)&=\frac{r^2+Nv-t+t\rho^N}{(N+1)^2}\\
&\ge f(N):=\frac{r^2+Nv-t}{(N+1)^2}.
\end{align*}
For real $x\ge1$, the derivative of $f$ has numerator $v-2(r^2-t)-vx$.
It can change sign from positive to negative, but never from negative to positive.
Consequently its minimum on $[1,N_{\max}]$ is attained at an endpoint.
Including stop yields the all-prefix lower bound
\[
\min_{0\le N\le N_{\max}}R(N)\ge\min\{r^2,f(1),f(N_{\max})\}.
\]
The bound also holds for a prior average by using the prior averages of $v$ and $t$.
Unlike the earlier asymptotic constants, it requires no eventual-monotonicity threshold.
For the parameter box, the exact running-mean risk increases with $\theta$ and nonnegative $\lambda$ because every covariance entry and averaging weight is nonnegative.
Thus the same lower bound evaluated at $\theta_{j,-}$ and $\lambda_-=39/200$ bounds every allowed fixed risk below.
At each prior endpoint, minimize it over all sixteen programs using $N_{\max}=\min\{\lfloor16B/(8+q)\rfloor,\lfloor4B/b\rfloor\}-8$.
All budgets in the displayed calculation make these horizons positive.
The common worst initialization is $|e|=r$, so observation-independent fixed mixtures cannot improve on the minimum of these lower bounds.

\subsection{Paid continuation and the same-stream correction}
The probe charges $288$ messages and eighteen clock units.
For commitment $a=(q,b)$ the applied horizon is
\[
N_a=\min\{\lfloor(16B-288)/(8+q)\rfloor,\lfloor(4B-18)/b\rfloor\}-8.
\]
Let $u_{j,a}=(1+N_av_{j,+}(a))/(N_a+1)^2$, with $v_{j,+}$ evaluated at the upper variance and persistence endpoints, and set
$\kappa_a=[\rho_+/((N_a+1)(1-\rho_+))]^2$, where $\rho_+=(41/200)^b$.
Then $J_{j,a}^r\le u_{j,a}$ and $k_{j,a}^2\le\kappa_a$ uniformly.
The initialization cross term vanishes because the frozen likelihood decision is even in the contrasts under every allowed centered Gaussian law.
For the error event, $\mathbb E|m_j^2-s_j^2|=4\varphi(1)s_j^2\le\theta_{j,+}$.
The absolute continuing-state selection correction is therefore at most $\theta_{j,+}(\kappa_c+\kappa_w)$.
With directional bound $\delta$ and commitments $c_j,w_j$, a rational upper bound is
\[
U_j=(1-\delta)u_{j,c_j}+\delta\max\{u_{j,c_j},u_{j,w_j}\}
+\theta_{j,+}(\kappa_{c_j}+\kappa_{w_j}).
\]
It follows from $J_c+p(J_w-J_c)\le(1-\delta)J_c+\delta\max(J_c,J_w)$ for $p\le\delta$.
Use $\delta=1/10$ for the full box and $\delta=1/40$ with the exact reference parameters.

For a given prior $\pi$, let $L(\pi)$ be the minimum of the all-prefix lower bounds and $U(\pi)=(1-\pi)U_0+\pi U_1$.
The lower bound $L$ is concave in $\pi$: each action's bound is a minimum of affine functions, and minimizing over actions preserves that form.
The upper bound $U$ is affine.
If $U(\pi_i)\le(1-\epsilon)L(\pi_i)$ at both endpoints $\pi_i\in\{9/20,11/20\}$, concavity proves the same inequality throughout the interval.
Since $I_{\rm fix}\ge L$ and $R_{\rm paid}\le U$, this proves the uniform relative guarantee.
Every number defining these bounds is rational.

\subsection{Extending the comparison to predetermined resource cycles}
Constant programs are included as period-one words.
For a word $a_1,\ldots,a_\ell$, $1\le\ell\le3$, put $\rho_t=\lambda^{b_t}$ and let $z_t^*$ be the unique periodic solution of $z_t^*=\rho_t(1+z_{t-1}^*)$.
Existence follows because the full-cycle multiplier $\prod_t\rho_t<1$; solving its scalar affine fixed point gives rational $z_t^*$ at rational parameters.
Write $\bar u=\ell^{-1}\sum_tq_t^{-1}$ and $\bar z=\ell^{-1}\sum_tz_t^*$.
Define the cycle remainder for $0\le s<\ell$ by
\[
\epsilon_s=\sum_{t=1}^s(q_t^{-1}-\bar u)+2\theta\sum_{t=1}^s(z_t^*-\bar z).
\]
The finite-prefix covariance recurrence starts at $z_1=0$ and satisfies $z_t=\rho_t(1+z_{t-1})$ thereafter.
Thus $z_t=z_t^*-z_1^*\prod_{u=2}^t\rho_u$.
Its accumulated noise variance obeys
\begin{align*}
\sum_{t=1}^N[q_t^{-1}+\theta(1+2z_t)]&\ge Nv-C,\\
v&=\bar u+\theta(1+2\bar z),\\
C&=\frac{2\theta z_1^*}{1-\max_t\rho_t}-\min_s\epsilon_s.
\end{align*}
The bound uses a geometric upper bound on the missing initial-cycle covariance and retains every cycle phase.
Replacing $t$ by $C$ in the preceding endpoint argument gives a lower bound over all prefixes of this cycle.

Let $m_s=\sum_{t=1}^s(8+q_t)$ and $e_s=\sum_{t=1}^sb_t$, including $m_0=e_0=0$.
Define the number of affordable complete cycles before residue $s$ by
\[
C_s(B)=\min\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{16B-m_s}{m_\ell}\right\rfloor,
\left\lfloor\frac{4B-e_s}{e_\ell}\right\rfloor\right\}.
\]
The maximal scheduled prefix and applied horizon are exactly
\begin{align*}
M_{\max}&=\max_{0\le s<\ell}\{\ell C_s(B)+s\},\\
N_{\max}&=M_{\max}-8.
\end{align*}
Every residue is feasible for the displayed budgets.
Apply the lower bound to all $4368$ words, using the lower parameter endpoints as before.
The first-prefix lower bounds are positive for every word at both prior endpoints in this calculation.
Each resulting lower bound remains a minimum of affine functions of the prior, so the same endpoint argument proves uniformity on $[0.45,0.55]$.
The fixed comparator is permitted to know the actual parameter pair and prior, but not the realized regime or initial error.
Its observation-independent mixtures cannot improve on the minimum prefix risk because their worst initialization is the same scalar endpoint.
The comparison is stronger than constant participation and spacing, while its declared period bound remains explicit.
