1. Conditional loss and the price of classifying a regime

Moustakides, Jajamovich, Tajer and Wang, Joint Detection and Estimation:
Optimum Tests and Applications (2012 journal metadata; inspected 2011v1
preprint https://arxiv.org/html/1101.5084), Section II Eqs.(3)--(9),
Lemma1 and SectionII-A Theorem1: conditional expected estimation cost and
detection decisions are already coupled. Minimizing a posterior loss is
not a new V3 decision principle. Their SectionI also separates problems
where detection is itself a desired output from nuisance-only inference.
V3 uses the regime only to choose a subsequent paid learner, and must state
that different objective rather than claim that joint inference was missing.
The specialized objects to establish are exact remaining-horizon continuing
loss, initialization-information restrictions, reflection gap and paid
useful/useless boundaries. These are not equivalent to a new JDE framework.

2. Observation-dependent allocation and risk-region selection

Vasconcelos and Mitra, Observation-Driven Scheduling for Remote Estimation
of Two Gaussian Random Variables (2020 journal; inspected 2018v1 titled
two Gaussian sources, https://arxiv.org/html/1804.01709), SectionII
Problem1 and SectionIV Lemma3, Eqs.(19)--(23), already choose an observation
region by pointwise estimation cost. Their scheduler affects the estimator's
information, explicitly creating selection coupling. Thus neither Gaussian
conditioning, observation-triggered selection nor an adaptive-over-fixed
advantage is new in general. V3's scope is a continuing frozen learner with
lost training budget, common-state carryover, and an unknown initial error.
The two-slot witness is only a specialized exact boundary; it must retain
its restrictive learner and information assumptions.

3. Robust JDE and minimax interchange

Reinhard, Fauss and Zoubir, Minimax Optimal Procedures for Joint Detection
and Estimation, arXiv:2604.22740v1 (preprint, not a verified journal article),
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22740, SectionsIII--IV-A Theorem2 and V-A
Theorem4: minimax JDE under uncertainty in parameter-conditional densities
already exists. Their Bayesian result compares integrated conditional losses;
the NP-like result adds error constraints. V3 must not claim a general new
minimax or robust joint-inference theorem. Its candidate covariance dual
instead convexifies a fixed unknown initialization vector, with specified
Gaussian observation laws and a frozen finite program catalogue. This is
a different uncertainty object, not evidence of broad priority by itself.

Sion, On general minimax theorems, Pacific J.Math.8(1),171--176 (1958),
https://msp.org/pjm/1958/8-1/pjm-v8-n1-p14-s.pdf, Section2 definitions,
Theorem3.4 and following corollary: the interchange is classical. The V3
matrix derivation must verify weak-star compactness, integrability and
continuity of its actual selector game. It must compare against fixed random
mixtures and preserve the positive-measure tie counterexample; choosing an
arbitrary pure minimizer at a dual optimizer need not produce a saddle rule.
No vector empirical advantage is established by the scalar studies.

4. Unknown target and error contrasts

Harville, Bayesian inference for variance components using only error
contrasts, Biometrika61(2),383--385 (1974), DOI10.1093/biomet/61.2.383.
Publisher metadata: https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/61/2/383/268084.
Primary article course copy: https://www.math.ntnu.no/emner/TMA4315/2020h/Harville-1974.pdf.
Read scope so far: printed384 Section2 Eqs.(1)--(3), Section3 and reference
continuation385. Printed383 still requires local visual reading; do not call
the entire article read. The contrast likelihood and determinant factor
involving X' Sigma^-1 X are already explicit classical results. They cannot
be presented as a new target-elimination theorem. V3's candidate addition
is their exact continuing-state interface, linear-time paired innovations
implementation, finite-nuisance affinity overhead and recomputed paid risk
certificate. Its information guarantee is invariant-statistic scoped, not
optimality over all raw-data estimators or unknown covariance laws.

5. What may enter the manuscript after proof/evidence gates

The scalar unknown-initialization envelope, matrix covariance dual, observable
continuing-state recursion and finite state-value ceiling are specialized
derivations built from the above tools. Full-text comparison supports this
attribution and difference in objects, not an exhaustive absence claim.
The signed decomposition explicitly distinguishes nominal posterior scores
from actual continuing risk; a first draft that conflated them was rejected
and preserved in value_decomposition_failure_01.json before use.

The current conditional2h study is frozen and validates only the known-zero
calibration-pilot setting. It cannot establish unknown-target observability.
The prospective observable1h study strengthens the baseline to every
affordable fixed training prefix, not only maximal-budget programs. Its
grid, cap and seed count are not enlarged to seek a favorable result.
If its public gate identically falls back everywhere, no redundant science
is needed to prove that identity. No negative result may be relabeled as
independent validation of a different selector.

6. Sequential estimation cost and asymptotic optimality

Reinhard, Fauss and Zoubir, Asymptotically optimal procedures for sequential
joint detection and estimation, Signal Processing219,109410(2024),
DOI10.1016/j.sigpro.2024.109410; inspected accepted preprint v3 dated1Feb2024,
https://arxiv.org/html/2105.04828v3. Read Section2.1 A1--A6,
Sections2.2--2.4 Eqs.(2)--(6), Section3 Theorems1,2 and displayed proof.
Its objective minimizes expected run length subject to separately controlled
hypothesis error and on-correct-decision parameter MSE. Its stopping objective
already prices observations and uses posterior losses. Its asymptotic limit
sends tolerated errors to zero. V3's fixed-budget resource-program comparison
and shared-state continuing loss are different objects; they do not license
a claim that inference cost or asymptotic joint decision theory is new.
The prior search-lead-only status is superseded for these inspected passages,
not for unrelated numerical examples or all supplement proofs.

Reinhard and Zoubir, (Sequential) Joint Detection and Estimation: Classic
Results and New Directions, arXiv2608.26278v1,26Aug2026,
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26278, is an overview preprint. Read
Sections2.2--2.3 Eqs.(6)--(8),P1; Section3.3 Eqs.(28)--(29);
Sections4.1--4.3. It documents classical posterior-loss optimization,
cost-controlled sequential stopping and robust-JDE work. Its general contrast
between exponentially decaying detection error and inverse-sample estimation
error is not V3's specific state-value O(B^-2) claim. V3 does not solve its
broad open problems concerning general robust/sequential data models.
Neither inspected source provides broad priority clearance for the new
resource-program specialization; their overlap must be acknowledged locally.
