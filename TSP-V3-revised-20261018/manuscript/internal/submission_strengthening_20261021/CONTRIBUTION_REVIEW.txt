Question: what cannot be replaced by an inexpensive fixed sampling program?
Answer must be conditional, not universal. A well-chosen fixed prefix can win
when identification consumes too much horizon, when covariance models share a
leading action, or when only the current noise state changes the decision.

PROPOSITION-BY-PROPOSITION DISPOSITION

1. Posterior conditional-loss minimization.
Classical: Moustakides et al.2012 SectionII/Lemma1/Theorem1; Vasconcelos and
Mitra2020 SectionIV/Lemma3; Reinhard et al.2026 SectionsIII--V.
No novelty claimed for conditional argmin, joint inference, Gaussian
conditioning, observation-based scheduling, or generic adaptive-over-fixed gain.
Specialized retained object: scalar initialization uncertainty shared by all
probe outcomes, exact paid continuing-program risk, and the invariant-statistic
policy class. Endpoint averaging gives a lower bound; an even first minimizer
cancels the odd term and attains it. This is not minimax over unknown models,
unknown priors, or all raw-observation estimators.

2. State-information value ceiling and paid-value decomposition.
Classical ingredients: conditional losses, reflection symmetry, Gaussian
absolute centered-square moment and the value-of-information principle.
Specialized retained result: the entire extra value beyond the regime posterior
is bounded by 4*phi(1)*range(k^2)*sum(pi*s^2), with O(B^-2) behavior for the
declared averaged learners, common persistence, uniform stability and horizons
proportional to B. The paid gain additionally contains the signed transport
term T; nominal posterior score is NOT actual same-stream risk. A draft omitting
T was rejected and archived, not included in the paper. No empirical claim of
useful state selection is licensed by optimality alone. The completed negative
conditional2h study is retained and this avenue is not tuned or retried.

3. First-order separation from all fixed training prefixes.
Classical ingredients: stationary long-run variance, finite-catalogue minima,
decision-error weighting, and asymptotic risk expansion.
Specialized retained result: under an explicit finite running-mean catalogue,
positive dual-budget ray, nonzero private variance, bounded delay and persistence
bounded below one, Delta>delta*A certifies a leading gain against EVERY fixed
affordable prefix and observation-independent random mixture, not just a weak
all-agent baseline. Eventual monotonicity plus strictly positive finite-prefix
risk closes the stronger prefix benchmark. Same-stream terms are second order.
The rational public-grid witness has positive asymptotic margin, not a validated
finite threshold or empirical effect. The no-first-order-gain boundary covers
only maximal-horizon post-probe programs plus stop and sublinear probe length,
not all sequential sensing or estimator strategies. Uniform remainder control
depends on the fixed finite catalogue and spectral assumptions.
The additional explicit-constant construction was derived during the live
observable1h analysis, before its outcomes were read, but AFTER the earlier
conditional2h negative was known. It is not retrospectively labeled pre-data.
It bounds all prefixes using an exact geometric risk identity, a finite-prefix
initialization lower bound and an explicit rounding loss, then bounds the
directional-error and continuing-state terms. Its conservative sufficient
threshold must not be confused with an observed finite-budget effect.
