# Same-stream risk-transfer reanalysis

This is an outcome-known analytical reanalysis, not a new preregistration or independent confirmation. The completed EXP-016B results are already known. No new trajectory, random sample, seed, fitted parameter, selected scenario, admission threshold or experimental gate is introduced.

## Fixed coverage and inputs

Retain all 104 registered scenarios, all eight policies, every registered budget point and both regimes. Layer A covers 104 scenarios; layer B covers its original 56. Preserve the eight search-censored scenarios. Use the original formal registry, reproduction audit and source hashes. Read first-seed public protocol metadata only to recover deterministic budget/action mappings, and use the already aggregated 192-seed cell summaries for empirical contrasts. Validate these mappings against frozen source arithmetic, not the first seed's return. Hash the full formal metrics before use.

## New analytical question

A probe and its downstream learner use disjoint observations but consecutive portions of the same stationary Gaussian AR(1) common-factor path. Derive the exact posterior-dependent squared risk of each frozen downstream linear program, then bound the decision-event correlation. Compute both one-sided corrections and intervals without assuming independent probe/learning paths or bounded conditional Gaussian histories. Recover exact linear weights for the running mean and the actual averaged FIFO affine learner, including paid withheld updates. A deterministic operator check supports implementation, but is not itself the research result.

## Reporting

Report every policy's analytic risk interval; all information-only minus learning-aware contrasts; below-identification, intermediate, above-value and search-censored strata; and the original practical-intermediate subset without changing its definition. Compare analytical population statements with saved empirical means descriptively. A sample mean outside a population-risk interval is not a theorem violation. Use no new significance claims, no sample-point coverage guarantee and no retrospective gate replacement. Count inconclusive and adverse intervals as well as positive ones. Preserve the original experiment and all prior failed studies.

## Resource and manuscript boundary

The analysis is local single-threaded linear algebra on completed artifacts, separate from the sole live native MARL owner. No environment call, sampler or old experiment/analyzer entry point may execute. New files have a separate output root. Original V3 and frozen evidence remain byte-identical. Manuscript admission requires a checked derivation, complete coverage, fresh citation integrity and IEEE style checks, compilation and visual inspection. Do not turn an analytical transfer result into a new native-MARL superiority claim.
