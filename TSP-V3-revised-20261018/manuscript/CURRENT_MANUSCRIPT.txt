Research direction
Multi-agent correlation changes the useful amount and timing of data. The
Lyapunov score prices participation, Markov spacing, delay and affordable horizon.
Paid identification is valuable when learning the covariance regime changes that
resource choice enough to repay its cost on the continuing learning stream.

New integrated results
1. Initialization-minimax/state-value characterization now permits regime-specific
   persistence, rather than requiring a common persistence across regimes.
2. An explicit two-program Gaussian construction attains strictly positive
   second-order state value, proving the O(B^-2) order sharp in that class.
3. A period-free resource-envelope theorem now covers EVERY predetermined
   schedule over the resource catalogue, including arbitrary nonperiodic words,
   all stopping prefixes and observation-independent randomized schedules.
   The unchanged paid rule guarantees at least 1.36%,4.89%,5.72% lower risk at
   reference B=512,2048,8192, and at least0.43% throughout the declared variance/
   persistence/prior box at B=8192. These are population-risk certificates, not
   sampled gains. The previous sharper period<=3 numbers remain supplementary.

Editorial judgment
Use this as the sole working main draft for a theory-led IEEE TSP submission.
Its substantive case is paid continuing-risk resource control and constructive
separation, not a claim that Lyapunov analysis, Bayesian decisions, Gaussian
contrasts, or information value are themselves new. The finite-model and declared
comparator classes are part of the theorem, not hidden assumptions.
Existing resource-matched learning gains remain in the main paper. Earlier
unfavorable prospective outcomes retain their original interpretation and full
supplementary evidence; the new construction does not relabel those experiments.
No new trajectories, seeds, native studies or manuscript submission were used.
