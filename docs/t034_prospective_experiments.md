# T-034 prospective theorem-testing experiments

## Purpose

The experiments test the theorem's phase classification.  They do not search
for a controller that wins every cell.  Every stochastic confirmation must be
registered after the relevant theorem constants, implementation hashes,
cells, metrics, and directions are frozen.

## Stage A: exact CPU phase map

Use exact or numerically certified affine/finite-state recursions over:

- independent and equicorrelated streams;
- two certified temporal mixing levels;
- no, moderate, and high delay;
- message-, environment-, and wall-clock resource rays;
- short, transition, and long horizons;
- the complete public participation catalogue including `q=1` and all-agent.

For each cell, the theorem must label speedup, saturation, or reversal before
the exact solution is evaluated.  Mandatory gates are at least 95% exact
classification, zero invalid bounds, nonvacuous risk intervals, and coverage
of all three regimes in every resource family where the theorem predicts
them.

## Stage B: standard tabular CPU confirmation

Use at least two public finite Markov tasks with exact kernels and one
continuing stochastic task.  The principal estimand is resource-matched
normalized mean-squared value-error area under the curve; terminal error and
tail risk are secondary.  Fresh common-random-number seeds must be assigned
after preregistration.

Mandatory gates:

- at least 80% of cells match the predicted direction;
- at least one standard task contains a positive regime with at least 5%
  improvement over the registered strong fixed comparator;
- the same task family contains a negative regime with at most 1% oracle
  value;
- the empirical transition budget lies inside the theorem's threshold
  sandwich;
- below-threshold fallback rate is at least 90%;
- above-threshold adaptation has a positive simultaneous confidence bound;
- no message, environment, delay, or wall-clock accounting violation.

Comparators include `q=1`, all-agent, best fixed-q, best fixed-subset when
identities differ, and a descriptive oracle.  The controller cannot use
outcome-fitted constants.

## Stage C: nonlinear robustness

Only after Stages A and B pass may a new nonlinear experiment be registered.
Its claim is qualitative regime ordering, not nonlinear convergence or exact
theorem calibration.  A GPU is justified only at this stage; until then every
task is local CPU work.

## Reuse rules

- EXP-018B may support the already registered fixed-parameter nonlinear
  covariance identity.
- EXP-016B may support the registered synthetic/affine threshold result.
- T-032 is exact deterministic evidence for a no-value region.
- Earlier pilot seeds and outcome-selected cells cannot become T-034
  confirmatory evidence.
- New predictions, hashes, and seeds require a separate preregistration
  commit before any Stage A or B outcome is generated.
