# Packet-debt Lyapunov control for asynchronous MARL

Date: 2026-09-06

Status: proved conditional event-time theorem and exact low-complexity launch
minimizer.  The theorem assumes a valid packet bias/variance certificate and
a uniformly accurate receipt score; construction of the deep-critic receipt
certificate remains open.  No new learning experiment is authorized here.

## 1. The single mechanism

Every asynchronously launched rollout creates a future liability: by the time
it returns, its policy gradient was generated by an old mixed-version joint
policy.  The central design principle is:

> Price a rollout's certified bias and variance when it is born, grow that
> debt under intervening policy updates, and redeem the same debt when the
> packet is consumed.

This gives one causal role to all online variables.  At dispatch, rollout
horizon `H` and policy-version refresh graph `E` minimize new packet debt plus
the communication price.  At receipt, the applied mass `alpha` minimizes the
remaining composite drift.  No launch decision uses the trajectory that it is
about to create.

The implementation model is owner-sharded asynchronous CTDE.  Learner/worker
`i` runs a joint simulator from its local policy-version cache and returns the
gradient block for owner `i`.  An edge `j -> i` sends policy block `j` to that
owner shard.  A batched launch for all owners in an active interaction region
has the union of these incoming edges; this is the complete directed graph on
the active region used by PDSG-CONE-001.  Execution remains decentralized and
uses none of these training edges.

## 2. Packet certificate at birth

Let packet `p` own block `i_p`, have rollout horizon `H_p`, mixed policy
version `zeta_p`, and a step cap `w_p`.  Just before revealing its update half
at receipt event `r`, assume

\[
\mathbb E[g_p\mid\mathcal G_r]
=\nabla_{i_p}F(\theta^r)+b_{p,r},
\qquad
\mathbb E[\|g_p-\mathbb E[g_p\mid\mathcal G_r]\|^2
\mid\mathcal G_r]\le\sigma_p^2.
\tag{1}
\]

Here `G_r` contains the current parameters and an optional fully charged
control split, but not the independent update half.  Continuing-chain data
require a martingale or regenerative replacement for this conditional split.

Decompose the conditional mean bias into `M_p` certified vector components:

- Markov-start, critic, horizon-truncation, and causal-cone terms; and
- for every sensitive policy block `j`, a version term bounded by
  `L_(p,j)||theta_j^r-zeta_(p,j)||`.

Cauchy--Schwarz gives the observable upper bound

\[
\|b_{p,r}\|^2\le
\mathcal Z_{p,r}^2
:=M_p\left[
\sum_{s\in\mathcal B_p}\beta_{p,s}^2
+\sum_{j\in C_p}L_{p,j}^2
\|\theta_j^r-\zeta_{p,j}\|^2
\right].
\tag{2}
\]

The causal-cone term may use the proved
`4 B_R B_Z delta_cone` bound.  A refresh dispatched before rollout birth has a
post-refresh radius that includes the certified source motion during bounded
communication delay.  If no finite delay/path bound is available, its fresh
radius is infinite and the edge cannot receive fictitious refresh credit.

Attach to packet `p` the history energy

\[
\Gamma_{p,r}
=\frac{5w_p}{4}\mathcal Z_{p,r}^2
+\frac{L_{i_p}w_p^2}{2}\sigma_p^2.
\tag{3}
\]

The coefficients in (3) are not tuned weights.  They are the constants needed
by the receipt cancellation in Section 4.

## 3. Exact launch-time horizon--graph control

At dispatch, a predictable upper bound on (3) at rollout birth is available
for every allowed integer horizon.  For edge `e`, let `r_e^0(H)` and
`r_e^1(H)` be its stale and post-refresh component radii.  For a fixed `H`,
refreshing `e` reduces the certified debt by

\[
\Delta_e(H)=\frac{5w_HM_H}{4}
\left[(r_e^0(H))^2-(r_e^1(H))^2\right].
\tag{4}
\]

With resource queues `Q_(r,k)`, learning weight `V`, and edge-resource costs
`c_(e,r)`, the exact launch rule is

\[
e\in E_k(H)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
V\Delta_e(H)>\sum_rQ_{r,k}c_{e,r}.
\tag{5}
\]

Under a cardinality cap, take the largest positive net values in (5).  The
controller then chooses the horizon with the smallest

\[
V\overline\Gamma_k(H,E_k(H))
+\sum_rQ_{r,k}c_{r,k}(H,E_k(H)).
\tag{6}
\]

Equations (4)--(6) are an exact minimizer because the squared bias certificate
in (2) is additive over its components.  A finite horizon set is the declared
integer action space of the online controller, not an offline hyperparameter
search.  The work is
`O(sum_(H in Hset)|C(X_k,H)|)` without a cap and adds sorting only when a
cardinality cap is imposed.  No Hessian inverse, covariance matrix, generic
QP, or per-edge environment probe is required.  The base resource vector
includes the `H` actor transitions consumed by the rollout; policy bytes are
charged edgewise.  Wall-clock occupancy may be added as a third measured
resource but is not the definition of learning quality.

The horizon tradeoff is endogenous.  Longer trajectories may reduce Markov,
truncation, or gradient-variance components but enlarge the causal cone and
allow more policy motion before receipt.  PDSG-CONE-001 independently confirms
the cone-densification side of this tradeoff on Pistonball.

## 4. Receipt cancellation lemma

Let `a=grad_i F(theta^r)`, `Z=Z_(p,r)`, `L=L_i`, `w=w_p`, and suppose

\[
Lw\le\frac14.
\tag{7}
\]

Remove the completing packet's energy (3), then apply its independent update
half with the full-cap comparison step `w`.  If no other packet were pending,
block smoothness and (1) imply

\[
\mathbb E_r[\Delta F-\Gamma_{p,r}]
\le-\frac w2\|a\|^2.
\tag{8}
\]

### Proof

Writing `mu=a+b`, block smoothness gives

\[
\mathbb E_r\Delta F
\le-w\langle a,a+b\rangle
+\frac{Lw^2}{2}(\|a+b\|^2+\sigma_p^2).
\tag{9}
\]

Use
`-<a,b> <= ||a||^2/4+||b||^2`,
`||a+b||^2 <= 2||a||^2+2||b||^2`,
`||b||<=Z`, and (7).  The right-hand side of (9) is at most

\[
-\frac w2\|a\|^2
+\frac{5w}{4}Z^2
+\frac{Lw^2}{2}\sigma_p^2.
\tag{10}
\]

Subtracting (3) proves (8).

Now retain all other in-flight packets.  Moving block `i` by a vector of norm
`u` changes their total history energy by at most

\[
h_{i,r}u+\frac{\kappa_{i,r}}2u^2,
\tag{11}
\]

where the additive certificate (2) gives the explicit coefficients

\[
\begin{aligned}
h_{i,r}&=\frac52\sum_{q\ne p}
w_qM_qL_{q,i}^2\|\theta_i^r-\zeta_{q,i}\|,\\
\kappa_{i,r}&=\frac52\sum_{q\ne p}w_qM_qL_{q,i}^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

If also `kappa_(i,r) w <= 1/8`, then the full-cap comparator obeys

\[
\mathbb E_r\Delta(F+\mathcal H)
\le-\frac w4\|a\|^2+\mathcal R_{p,r},
\tag{13}
\]

with

\[
\mathcal R_{p,r}
=2wh_{i,r}^2+wh_{i,r}(Z+\sigma_p)
+\kappa_{i,r}w^2Z^2
+\frac{\kappa_{i,r}w^2}{2}\sigma_p^2.
\tag{14}
\]

To prove (13), use
`E||g_p|| <= ||a||+Z+sigma_p` in the linear part of (11) and
`E||g_p||^2 <= 2||a||^2+2Z^2+sigma_p^2` in its quadratic part.  Then apply
`h||a|| <= ||a||^2/8+2h^2`; the condition on `kappa w`
pays one additional `w||a||^2/8`.  Together with (8), the remaining descent
coefficient is `w/4` and the non-gradient terms are exactly (14).

The actual receipt controller minimizes a predictable estimate of the convex
scalar drift bound over `0 <= alpha <= w`.  Since `alpha=w` is feasible, the
noisy-minimization lemma makes its true conditional drift no larger than
(13) plus twice the uniform receipt-score error.  The algorithm may therefore
choose less than `w` without weakening the comparison theorem.

## 5. Finite-time learning and communication theorem

Let

\[
\Phi_k=F(\theta^k)-F_\star
+\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_k}\Gamma_{p,k}
+\sum_r\frac{Q_{r,k}^2}{2V},
\tag{15}
\]

and update `Q_(r,k+1)=[Q_(r,k)+c_(r,k)-bar c_r]^+` only at launch events.
Assume resource `r` has costs in `[0,c_(r,max)]`.  At each launch, the
algorithm minimizes (6) with conditional uniform packet-debt error
`epsilon_k^L`.  Let any predictable comparison policy have conditional
average cost at most `bar c_r` for every resource and birth debt
`Gamma_k^circ`.  At every receipt, assume (1), (7), the condition below (12),
and conditional uniform scalar-score error `epsilon_r^R`.

For any event prefix containing `N` launches and a set `R` of receipts,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac14\sum_{p\in\mathcal R}
\mathbb E[w_p\|\nabla_{i_p}F(\theta^{r(p)})\|^2]
\le{}&\Phi_0\\
&+\sum_{k=1}^{N}\mathbb E\left[
\Gamma_k^\circ+2\epsilon_k^L+\frac{B_Q}{V}\right]\\
&+\sum_{p\in\mathcal R}\mathbb E\left[
\mathcal R_{p,r(p)}+2\epsilon_{r(p)}^R\right],
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]

where `B_Q=sum_r c_(r,max)^2/2`.

### Proof

At launch, only the new packet history and queue change.  The half-square
queue inequality, divided by `V`, and the noisy minimizing property compare
the selected birth drift to the budget-feasible comparator with penalty
`2 epsilon_k^L+B_Q/V`.  At receipt, apply (13) and the same noisy minimizing
property.  All other packet-history changes are already in (11)--(14).  Sum
over the chronological launch/receipt filtration, telescope (15), and use
`Phi_terminal >= 0`.  This proves (16).  A packet still pending at the end
only leaves nonnegative energy in `Phi_terminal`; it is not silently treated
as completed.

Pathwise queue iteration gives, for every resource `r`,

\[
\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^Nc_{r,k}
\le\bar c_r+\frac{Q_{r,N}-Q_{r,0}}{N}.
\tag{17}
\]

For the message resource, if a zero-refresh action exists, each positive
message costs at least `c_min`, and every estimated birth debt has magnitude
at most `M_Gamma`, then

\[
Q_{\mathrm{msg},k}
\le\frac{2VM_\Gamma}{c_{\min}}+c_{\mathrm{msg},\max}.
\tag{18}
\]

For a mandatory resource such as environment transitions, the corresponding
pathwise budget needs either a feasible low-cost horizon action plus the same
bounded-score comparison, or a standard Slater condition.  Thus graph and
horizon are chosen by multi-resource Lyapunov backpressure; each claimed
budget must state the condition that bounds its own queue.

For a bounded number of in-flight packets, bounded certificates, constant
`w=N^(-1/2)`, and `V=N`, (16)--(18) yield an `O(N^(-1/2))` optimization/budget
remainder plus the comparison policy's average squared mean-bias floor and
normalized receipt-score error.  The variance contribution is `O(w)`.  A
vanishing stationarity guarantee therefore requires the comparator bias and
receipt estimation error to vanish; the theorem does not rename a fixed
neighborhood as exact convergence.

## 6. Why this closes the launch--receipt mismatch

The dispatch decision does not predict the exact future return gain.  It
controls the debt inserted into the Lyapunov state at packet birth.  Every
intervening accepted update changes that same state through (11), and packet
receipt removes the current value before the stochastic step.  Consequently
random launch/receipt ordering is handled by chronological telescoping.  A
bounded communication flight delay enters the birth certificate; rollout and
queue delays enter the version distances in (2) and the interference terms in
(12).  No wall-clock superiority assumption is needed.

This replaces the earlier proposed launch-time signed-JVP edge score.  Signed
alignment is still needed for the scalar receipt action, where current state
and a fully charged control split are actually available.  The graph itself
is selected from a structural causal cone and certified packet-risk reduction,
avoiding the failed return-only graph-discovery route.

## 7. Controlled-SDDE role

A controlled hybrid SDDE can represent the large-system phase limit: launches
add atoms to a measure-valued packet-debt state, intervening policy jumps age
those atoms, and receipts remove atoms while applying controlled parameter
jumps.  Applying an Itô formula to the continuous analogue of (15) recovers
the three drift terms that generate `H`, `E`, and `alpha` decisions.

The exact guarantee in this document is nevertheless discrete and event-time.
The paper may use the SDDE to characterize delay/noise phase boundaries only
after proving a weak-approximation error for the hybrid event process.  SDDE is
not needed to make (16) correct and will not be advertised as the source of a
guarantee that it has not proved.

## 8. Remaining gates before a learning runner

1. Construct a time-uniform or expectation-uniform receipt score from a
   centralized critic/control split and bound its Markov/off-policy error.
2. Instantiate the bias components, cross-sensitivities, bounded delay, and
   variance certificate in an exact factored Markov game.
3. Show outcome-free oracle headroom over the strongest fixed
   horizon/static-graph policy after all messages and control/update samples
   are charged.
4. Only if the above pass, freeze a new CPU controller experiment with a
   strong fixed envelope, no-refresh, always-fresh, age-based, and oracle
   comparators plus nontriviality and reproducibility gates.
