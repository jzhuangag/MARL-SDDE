# Preregistration: Two Clocks public-MPE CPU bridge pilot

Date: 2026-09-03.

## Status and scientific role

This is a fresh-seed **CPU feasibility pilot**, not paper-formal evidence.  It
tests whether the off-diagonal Lyapunov design survives a public neural MARL
interface strongly enough to justify a later upstream quality anchor and GPU
preregistration.  It does not repair or replace any prior failed experiment.

The frozen G0 Amendment 1 passed on commit `bed08d1`.  No G0 outcome was
generated.  The present pilot may be run only after this preregistration and
its runner, analyzer, configuration, seeds, and gates are committed.

## Unified hypothesis

With distinct CTDE policy blocks and one in-flight trajectory packet per
owner, a packet is self-fresh but strategically stale through teammate-policy
motion.  Under heterogeneous service clocks, removing the global barrier
should create extra useful query depth only when the off-diagonal interaction
history remains inside the Lyapunov--Krasovskii step domain.  Under balanced
clocks the benefit should vanish.  This is a rate--coupling phase hypothesis,
not a claim of universal asynchronous dominance.

## Frozen design

- Public tasks: continuous-action PettingZoo MPE `simple_spread_v2` and
  `simple_reference_v2` through pinned HARL commit
  `b1af98b0dbab72a2eee9d160751cd09aedbb8ce2`.
- Distinct actors: `[32, 32]` HARL stochastic-policy networks.
- Estimator: average of two independent, fully charged Monte Carlo policy
  directions with a 16-episode past-data, action-independent frozen control
  variate.  This is deliberately stronger than the old zero-baseline GPU
  pilot, but is not presented as full HAA2C.
- Methods: off-diagonal Lyapunov asynchronous, raw asynchronous,
  full-event-delay-scaled asynchronous, and fully utilized frozen-policy
  barrier.
- Service profiles: balanced and predeclared heterogeneous clocks.
- Eight new CRN seeds generated by
  `93,000,000 + uint32_be(SHA256('TWO-CLOCKS-MPE-PILOT:<index>')[0:4]) mod 5,000,000`.
- Five evaluation checkpoints at common logical-service fractions
  `{0,.25,.5,.75,1}`; five fixed evaluation episodes per checkpoint.
- Step: learning rate `5e-4`, radial packet-step cap `0.05`, fixed before any
  pilot outcome.
- Lyapunov matrix: diagonal `5`, off-diagonal `0.5`, pathwise event-delay
  bound `8`.  The candidate multiplier is the certified common step divided
  by the diagonal-only `1/L_ii` step; it is not tuned on pilot outcomes.

The task horizons are 128 and 192 service units.  They give 384 completed
packets in the balanced profile for both tasks.  Under heterogeneous clocks,
the three-agent task gives 242 asynchronous versus 224 complete barrier
packets; the two-agent task gives 240 for both but 240 asynchronous owner
updates versus 96 barrier-batched owner updates.  All incomplete tail work is
charged.

Frozen workload: 128 endpoint rows, 640 curve rows, 39,856 complete packets,
1,992,800 completed training environment steps, and 4,980,000 completed actor
transitions, plus separately reported baseline, evaluation, and partial work.
The estimate is suitable for four local CPU workers; neither GPU nor HPC4 is
authorized.

## Frozen outcomes and gates

The primary metric is time-normalized AUC of evaluation return.  For a paired
cell, the normalized AUC difference divides by `max(1, abs(initial_return))`.

Mandatory gates P1--P9 and P11--P12 are exactly those stored in the frozen
configuration.  In brief: exact validity/pairing/accounting; positive learning
for every task-method aggregate; at least 0.05 aggregate heterogeneous
off-diagonal-versus-barrier normalized AUC gain and positive gain on both
tasks; at least 60% paired directionality; no more than 0.02 aggregate or
taskwise loss to the stronger raw/full-delay asynchronous reference; a larger
heterogeneous than balanced gain on each task; nontrivial theorem-derived
motion; full motion/work disclosure; no outcome-selected setting; no formal
or GPU escalation; and byte-identical scientific reproduction.

P10 is a separate public-quality gate: a pinned upstream HAA2C learnability
anchor must be executed before any GPU preregistration.  It is intentionally
not required to read this pilot, so it cannot rescue a failed bridge.

Any mandatory pilot failure permanently stops this configuration.  Seeds,
tasks, horizons, methods, estimator, constants, and thresholds may not be
modified after outcome access.  A pass permits the separate HAA2C anchor; it
does not turn pilot data into formal evidence.
